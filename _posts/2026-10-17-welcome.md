This is the new home for my work and writing. I'm Ryan J. Quinlan, a freelance Compositing Supervisor and Pipeline TD/Consultant with more than 19 years in visual effects, working across feature films, commercials, music videos and interactive experiences.

## What you'll find here

Notes from the place where compositing and pipeline meet:

- **Compositing:** techniques, workflows and Nuke.
- **Pipeline and tools:** building artist-friendly tools for desktop and the cloud, and bridging the gap between artists and engineers.
- **Leading teams:** how to keep productions calm and efficient, and help people grow along the way.
- **Teaching:** lessons from my time as an Associate Professor at DePaul University and as a technical trainer and featured speaker for Foundry.
